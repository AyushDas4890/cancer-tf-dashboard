# Style guide: Cancer TF Atlas hero film

Source: `assets/site/info.json`, `assets/site/hero.png`, the redesigned site (local build). No reference film; grammar from the house rules.

## 1. Palette (measured from the site)
| Token | Hex | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | #06080B | site canvas (`bg-ink`) |
| `--ink` | #EDEFF2 | headlines |
| `--ink-2` | #A3ACB9 | body copy |
| `--accent` | #F2C14E | logo badge / primary button: the ONE accent |
| `--card` | #0C0F14 | panels |
Data hues (BRCA #e15084, KIRC #02a3c0, LUAD #1fb032, PRAD #7b5dc7, COAD #ad4303) are particles only, exactly as on the site. Type never wears them.

## 2. Type
- Display: Space Grotesk 500, tracking -0.045em, line-height 0.92, sentence case, full stops (site H1 habit).
- UI: JetBrains Mono 400–500 for eyebrows, numbers, labels (site's data face), uppercase eyebrows with wide tracking.
- 1080p sizes: hero 190–230 px, secondary 110–150 px, labels ≥ 30 px. Accent on the key phrase only ("master switches.").

## 3. Rhythm
120 BPM, bar = 2 s. Hard cuts on bars 8 / 16 / 24 / 36 (beats). Inner events on beats/half-beats. Nothing holds > 1 bar without a new element.

## 4. Transitions
Hard cut on the bar · particle morph (the site's own helix → matrix → sphere → clusters) · card rise · push-through. No crossfades.

## 5. Camera
Continuous slow orbit of the particle field (3D projected to a 2D canvas, deterministic) + micro push 1.00 → 1.05 on every hold.

## 6. Texture
Clean digital dark lab, faint static grain like the site. Depth from particle size/alpha falloff, never glow on UI.

## 7. Text in / out
Words rise through masks (heavy), leave upward (snappy). Eyebrows may fade. Swaps sequential.

## 8. Sound
Synth score Dm–Bb–F–C, intro → full → build → gap → drop on the cluster burst → full → outro. Pops on words/labels, ticks under the gene counter, whoosh on morphs, thump on cluster burst and logo, click on the predictor button.
